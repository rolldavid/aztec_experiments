# Direct social actions

Likes/unlikes, bookmarks/follows and read markers submit directly from the reader. Name, bio and avatar edits use one Create/Save. Headless reader actions capture the exact target and requested state before asynchronous work, share an active submission, preserve uncertain outcomes without resending, and open setup without automatically submitting after it. Confirmed bookmark changes immediately support the inverse action. Profile version, preference revision, name availability, exact payload and configured fee ceilings remain enforced.
